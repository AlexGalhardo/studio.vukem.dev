# Text cards

The default style. Each scene is one card: a flat background colour, a short title, and one to three lines of text. Cards cut one after another. There is no audio.

## Rules for the plan

- Use between 3 and 12 scenes. One idea per scene.
- Scene text is at most 120 characters. Break long sentences into more scenes.
- Pick a background colour per scene as `#RRGGBB`. Keep contrast high with the white text: use dark or saturated colours.
- The first scene hooks the viewer in under 3 seconds. The last scene carries the call to action.
- Scene seconds add up to the requested duration.
- Never state a claim that is not in the brief's must-keep facts.
