# Simple stroke

This is the English visual law for the compiler. The Portuguese kit stays beside it.

- Shapes are large, flat, and simple, like cut paper. No outline, no shadow, no gradient.
- Edges boil at 12 fps. The encoder may repeat those frames to 30 fps.
- Motion is a jump in, one gesture, and a whip out. The background cuts hard.
- The palette is short and saturated. One object at a time on a flat field.
- Frame size and duration come from the brief. Do not invent a second palette.
