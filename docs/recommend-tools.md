# Recommended tools

Translated from the Portuguese source list. Names are the products. The line under each name is the use the list gave them. Hosted products here are references. They are not dependencies of the Studio worker. See [Tools](../site/content/docs/tools/overview.mdx).

Affiliate calls to action from the source page are omitted.

## Language models

- **ChatGPT** — general model chat
- **Claude** — general model chat
- **ChatHub** — several models in one view
- **Grok** — xAI chat
- **Copilot** — editor assistant
- **Google Bard** — listed under its old name; the product is now Gemini
- **Replicate** — hosted model APIs
- **NatDev** — try more than one model

## Email marketing

- **Beehiiv** — newsletter product
- **ActiveCampaign** — marketing automation
- **LeadLovers** — marketing automation

## Capture and transcription of calls

- **tl;dv** — meeting notes and summaries
- **Fathom** — call summaries
- **Tactiq** — call transcription, with a free monthly tier
- **Fireflies** — call transcription
- **CleanShot** — screen capture
- **Loom** — screen and camera recording
- **Screenbrush** — draw on a Mac screen

## Avatar and voice

- **HeyGen** — avatar video
- **ElevenLabs** — speech synthesis and voice clone

## AI video editing

- **Opus Clip** — long video to short clips
- **Descript** — edit from the transcript
- **Submagic** — captions, b-roll, zoom, sound effects
- **Pictory** — text to video
- **Fliki** — text to video and speech
- **CapCut** — editor with some AI tools

## Audio and transcription

- **Auphonic** — loudness, cleanup, transcript
- **AssemblyAI** — transcription API
- **Pinpoint** — transcription and OCR
- **Voice In** — speech to text in the browser
- **Krisp** — noise removal on calls
- **ElevenLabs** — also listed here for a cloned voice

## Graphics

- **Canva** — layout; a bulk-create extension was mentioned for accounts that lack the paid bulk feature
- **Gamma** — decks from a prompt
- **Figma**
- **Miro**
