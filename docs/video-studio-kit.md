# Video studio kit

Translated from the Portuguese kit notes. This is the reference shelf and the local tool list the skill packs assume.

## Picture references

### Product and interface

- [21st.dev](https://21st.dev) — components
- Inspo — real sites, useful as a visual target for an agent
- Godly — high-craft marketing sites
- SaaSFrame — landing, pricing, onboarding
- Mobbin — product screens and flows
- Refero — interface patterns
- Page Flows — recorded signup, onboarding, and checkout
- One Page Love — small sites
- Awwwards — art direction and motion on the web
- SiteInspire — type and layout
- Dribbble — fast UI sketches
- Behance — full campaigns
- Savee — mood boards

### Motion and film

- What Ships — product and launch videos
- Raivcoo — motion, type, 3D, launch films
- Eyecandy — cuts grouped by technique (match cut, zoom, split screen)
- Directors' Library — commercials and music videos
- Framerate — motion showreel
- Stash — studio motion and 3D
- Motionographer — discovery
- Good Moves — experimental type and brand films
- Art of the Title — title sequences
- [FrameThrower](https://framethrower.ai/)

### Cinematography stills

- FrameSet — search by photography, ad, composition, mood
- ShotDeck — film frames by light, color, lens, framing
- FilmGrab — film frames

### Design systems named in the kit

- https://designmd.ai/
- https://www.designmd.co/

### One automated editor, as a reference

Revid generates social shorts. It is a product reference, not a library of stills, and not a Studio dependency.

## Shelves to open first

| Question | Open |
| --- | --- |
| Web look | Inspo, Godly, SaaSFrame, 21st.dev |
| Product flow | Mobbin, Refero, Page Flows |
| Video | What Ships, Raivcoo, Eyecandy |
| Art direction | Directors' Library, Stash, FrameSet or ShotDeck |
